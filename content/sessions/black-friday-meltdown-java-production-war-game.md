A gamified 90-minute hands-on work-a-thon where participants step into the shoes of a production support engineering team during a high-stakes Black Friday outage!

Rather than passive slides, attendees will troubleshoot a live, failing Java & Spring Boot application:
- Uncovering subtle AI-generated bugs and race conditions
- Debugging concurrency deadlocks and thread pool starvations
- Solving memory leaks and mysterious HashMap corruptions
- Restoring service health and validating system stability under load

Join this war game to sharpen your real-world Java debugging and incident response skills under pressure.

Note: Not your typical workshop. It’s a Work-a-Thon!
Think Hack-a-Thon, but with real-world crisis scenarios! The speaker will walk you through a crisis, challenge you to think on your feet, and guide everyone as you collaborate to solve the problem together.
