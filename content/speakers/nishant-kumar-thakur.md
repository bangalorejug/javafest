Nishant is a Senior Computer Scientist at Adobe, where he has worked across multiple products for the past 15 years. These days, he is architecting the Adobe.com agentic web platform and building and benchmarking agentic pipelines for Adobe's web infrastructure within the Edge Delivery Services ecosystem. He holds a patent and recently completed a Master of Data Science from Deakin University, Australia.

Nishant is an active member of the Bengaluru tech community. Recent engagements include the AI Agent Builder Series (where he served as a mentor), AgentCon Bengaluru (where he won second prize), the MLX India Community Meetup, GDG Bengaluru, and AI House.
