Rahul Mohan is a Product Manager at Digitap.ai in Bengaluru, working on the Bank Statement Analyser product. He specializes in product investigation, problem discovery, and working with AI coding tools to design verification and fraud-detection logic.

He was a national finalist at SAP HackFest 2025 for PalmPay, a UPI verification device built for BMTC bus conductors after 80-plus field interviews, and has pitched and presented at multiple national technology hackathons.
